# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary in plain English. The full text at that link applies where this summary is silent.

## Our pledge

We want everyone to be able to take part without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, language or sexual identity. The project supports English and Arabic, and contributors writing in either language are equally welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful. Assume good intent and give useful, specific feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for the community.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or attention, or unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or anything else that would be unprofessional in a shared workplace.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, comments) and anywhere someone is clearly representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a concern, use the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**. This opens a private advisory that only the maintainers can see. Please say clearly in the title that it is a conduct report, not a security issue. You can also contact a maintainer through GitHub.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn, temporarily block or permanently ban people who break these rules. We will keep reporters' details private and treat every report in good faith.
